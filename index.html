Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026  ·  Last updated: October 26, 2026

Noor Al-Islam (“the App”) offers prayer times, Quran reading and audio recitation, Qibla direction, Azkar, prayer reminders, and other Islamic tools. This policy explains what information the App may access, how it is used, and your choices.

1. Who operates the App

Noor Al-Islam is operated by Matin Khalid Shkur (“we,” “us,” or “our”). Privacy questions can be sent to nuralisllam@gmail.com.

2. Information the App uses

  • Location: With your permission, the App may access approximate or precise location to calculate local prayer times, determine Qibla direction, and show nearby mosques. Location may also be used to determine the applicable time zone. You can turn off location permission in Android Settings and may be able to select a location manually.
  • Notification preferences: The App uses your notification permission and prayer alert preferences to schedule Azan alerts, prayer-time reminders, and optional Azkar/Quran reminders.
  • App settings and activity: Language choices, calculation method, madhab, audio preferences, prayer tracking, reading position, and other preferences may be stored on your device so the App works as expected.
  • Audio and downloaded content: The App may access its own stored Quran audio or download/stream recitations. Playback status and downloaded content may be stored locally.
  • Device functionality: The App may use device orientation or compass sensors to provide Qibla direction. Background processing and alarms may be used to deliver scheduled prayer notifications.

Access to these features depends on the permissions you grant, your settings, and which tools you use.

3. Why information is used

Information is used to deliver requested features, personalize your experience, calculate prayer times and Qibla direction, play or download Quran audio, save your settings, deliver notifications, and maintain the App’s security and reliability.
